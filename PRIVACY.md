# Privacy Policy — OmniaDLC

**Last updated:** September 9, 2026

OmniaDLC (“the Client”, “we”) respects your privacy. This policy describes what information may be processed when you use the OmniaDLC Minecraft client and related Discord Rich Presence features.

## 1. Information we may process

Depending on features you enable, the Client may use:

- **Discord account display data** (username / user ID / avatar) solely to show Rich Presence status through the official Discord desktop application and Discord RPC.
- **Local configuration files** stored on your device (for example settings and configs in the Omnia folder).
- **Technical runtime data** needed for the game client to function (Minecraft session, FPS, server address shown in local HUD widgets).

We do **not** intentionally collect payment card data, government IDs, or biometric data.

## 2. How information is used

Data is used only to:

- display Discord Rich Presence (activity status);
- keep local client settings;
- provide in-game UI / HUD features.

## 3. Sharing

The Client does not sell your personal data.  
Discord-related display data is handled by Discord under Discord’s own privacy policy when Rich Presence is active.

## 4. Storage

Local files remain on your computer until you delete them.  
We do not operate a mandatory central account database for OmniaDLC Rich Presence itself.

## 5. Third-party services

If you use Discord, Minecraft, or linked community resources (Telegram / Discord invite links), their privacy policies apply to those services.

## 6. Your choices

You can:

- close Discord to stop Rich Presence;
- delete the Omnia config folder on your PC;
- stop using the Client at any time.

## 7. Contact

For privacy questions about OmniaDLC, contact the project maintainers through the community Discord / Telegram channels linked in the client.

## 8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.
